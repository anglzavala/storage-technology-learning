Digital Storage Fundamentals

Understanding digital storage

💾

What is digital storage?

Digital storage is any tool or system used to save data so it can be retrieved, copied, protected, or shared later.

⚡

Capacity and speed

Capacity tells us how much data fits, while speed tells us how quickly data can be read and written.

🛡️

Security and backups

Encryption and backups help protect information from loss, theft, and accidental damage.

📦

Portability and durability

Some devices are designed for travel and rugged use, while others focus on long-term storage or large-scale access.
